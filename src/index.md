I'm an Artificial Intelligence MSc student at [Ensimag - INP Grenoble](https://ensimag.grenoble-inp.fr/en).

I'm broadly interested in optimal and learning-based control, with special emphasis on robot learning. My research aims to make autonomous agents more generally capable and efficient by leveraging optimization and machine learning methods.

As an MSc student in Ensimag, I worked on multi-objective reinforcement learning with formal specifications with Prof. [Alexandre Donzé](https://www-verimag.imag.fr/PEOPLE/donzeal/) in the [Verimag laboratory](https://www-verimag.imag.fr/?lang=en).
Before coming to Grenoble, I obtained my BSc at the [Public University of Navarre](https://www.unavarra.es/home), where I worked with Prof. [Jesús Palacián](https://www.unavarra.es/pdi?uid=1715&languageId=1) on learning for control in robotics and astrodynamics.
I also worked with the [UpnaLab](https://upnalab.com/) research team led by Prof. [Asier Marzo](https://www.unavarra.es/pdi?uid=8600), mostly on simulations for acoustic holography.

Check out my projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/jongoiko), visit my [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jon-goikoetxea-5a47ab433/), or email me [here](mailto:jon.goikoetxea.m@gmail.com).
